Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how AppTime ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

🔒 Your Data Security & Privacy Promise:

All your data is securely transferred using industry-standard encryption protocols. Your personal information, app usage data, and any other data collected by our App will only be visible to others when you explicitly agree and give your consent. We respect your privacy and will never share your data without your explicit permission.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 App Usage Data

To provide screen time tracking and app usage monitoring features, we collect:

  • App names and package names you use
  • Time spent using each application
  • Daily and weekly screen time statistics
  • Network usage data (WiFi and cellular data consumption per app)

1.2 Device Information

We may collect certain device information to provide and improve our services:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version information
  • Network connection type (WiFi or cellular)

1.3 User Preferences

We store your app preferences locally on your device, including:

  • Theme preferences (light, dark, or system)
  • Language preferences
  • App blocking settings
  • Website blocking configurations

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Core Functionality: To track and display your screen time and app usage statistics
  • App Blocking: To implement app blocking features using VPN service
  • Website Blocking: To block access to specific websites when using the VPN service
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve app performance
  • Notifications: To send you usage alerts and reminders

3. VPN Service

AppTime uses Android's VPN service to implement app and website blocking features. When you enable blocking:

  • We create a local VPN connection on your device
  • Network traffic is routed through our VPN service to filter and block specific apps or websites
  • No network traffic data is transmitted outside your device
  • The VPN service runs locally and does not route your traffic through external servers

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All app usage data and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your usage statistics to external servers unless you explicitly enable cloud sync features.

4.2 Data Security and Secure Transfer

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data and ensure secure transfer:

  • Secure Data Transfer: All data transferred (if you enable cloud sync or data sharing features) is encrypted using industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to ensure your data is securely transmitted
  • Secure Storage: Data is stored securely on your device using Android's secure storage mechanisms
  • Encryption: We use encryption where applicable to protect your data at rest and in transit
  • Access Control: Access to usage data is restricted to your device only, and is only shared with others when you explicitly agree and provide consent

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google AdMob

Our App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information as described in their privacy policy. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Google Play Services

The App uses Google Play Services for various features. Google's privacy practices are governed by their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Permissions Required

The App requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Usage Statistics (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Required to track which apps you use and for how long
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to send usage alerts and reminders
  • VPN Service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE): Required to implement app and website blocking features
  • Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Required to access information about installed apps for usage tracking
  • Internet Access: Required for displaying ads and syncing data (if enabled)

7. Data Sharing and Consent

Your data will only be visible to others when you explicitly agree and give your consent. We respect your privacy and follow these principles:

  • No Sharing Without Consent: We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties without your explicit consent
  • Secure Transfer: When you choose to share data (e.g., through cloud sync or family sharing features), all data is securely transferred using encrypted connections
  • Anonymized Data: We may share anonymized, aggregated data for analytics purposes, but this data cannot be used to identify individual users and is only shared after removing all personally identifiable information
  • Consent Required: Any sharing of your personal data with third parties or other users requires your explicit agreement and consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time through the App settings
  • Transparency: We will clearly inform you before any data sharing occurs and require your explicit consent

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your usage data stored in the App
  • Delete your usage data at any time through the App settings
  • Revoke permissions granted to the App through your device settings
  • Uninstall the App, which will remove all locally stored data
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device's ad settings

10. Data Retention

We retain your usage data locally on your device for as long as you use the App. When you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is deleted. If you delete your data through the App settings, it is removed immediately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

13. Consent and Your Control

Your consent is essential for any data sharing. By using our App, you consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. However:

  • Local Data: Your usage data stored locally on your device does not require additional consent as it remains private to you
  • Data Sharing: Your data will only be visible to others when you explicitly agree and give your consent. We will never share your data without your explicit permission
  • Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent at any time through the App settings or by revoking permissions in your device settings
  • Right to Refuse: If you do not agree with this policy or any data sharing practices, please do not use the App or disable data sharing features in the App settings

This privacy policy is effective as of the date listed above and applies to all users of the AppTime application.

Child Safety & CSAE Policy

Published: October 6, 2026 • Effective Date: October 6, 2026 • Package: com.app.screentime

Winter (operated by Winter App Inc., package name com.app.screentime) maintains an absolute Zero-Tolerance Policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

ZERO-TOLERANCE MANDATE: Any depiction, facilitation, solicitation, sharing, or promotion of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) is strictly prohibited on Winter. We actively prevent, detect, investigate, and report all instances of CSAE to law enforcement and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
Imminent Danger: If you believe a child is in immediate danger or being harmed right now, immediately call your local emergency services (911 in the US/Canada, 112 in the EU/UK, 100/112 in India) or contact the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.

1. Strict 18+ Age Restriction & Age Verification

Winter is an adult-only dating and connection platform designed exclusively for individuals who are 18 years of age or older (or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction).

  • No Minors Permitted: Individuals under the age of 18 are legally prohibited from creating an account, browsing profiles, exchanging messages, or participating in WebRTC voice and video calls.
  • Mandatory Date of Birth Verification: During user registration, all applicants must declare their accurate date of birth. Accounts indicating an age under 18 are rejected immediately at onboarding.
  • Biometric & Live Selfie Verification: We deploy liveness detection and automated facial age estimation technologies during onboarding. Any profile suspected of being operated by a minor or misrepresenting adult age is blocked and flagged for human safety review.

2. Prohibited Content & Conduct (CSAE/CSAM)

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and international child protection laws, Winter strictly bans:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any visual, photographic, digital, video, audio, or computer-generated depiction of a minor engaged in sexually explicit or suggestive conduct.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE): Any act of grooming, sexual extortion (sextortion), sexual coercion, propositioning, or soliciting sexual activity with or involving a minor.
  • Sexualization of Minors: Sharing, uploading, transmitting, or requesting images, texts, voice notes, or videos that sexualize minors or suggest underage sexual behavior.
  • Child Grooming: Attempting to build an emotional connection with a minor to facilitate sexual contact, exploitation, or abuse.
  • Trafficking: Any commercial sexual exploitation, trafficking, or prostitution involving children or youths.
No Intent Defense: We treat any sharing, posting, transmitting, or requesting of CSAE/CSAM as an intentional, critical violation. Claims of joke, parody, fictional AI generation, or research will not be tolerated.

3. Preventative Safeguards & Proactive Detection

To prevent the proliferation of CSAE and protect minors, Winter implements layered technical, algorithmic, and human safeguards:

  • Automated Media Screening: All photos uploaded to profiles or shared across chats are screened against known digital hash databases (such as PhotoDNA / PDQ hashing) and real-time computer vision classifiers for explicit material.
  • Real-Time Text Safety Filters: Natural language processing (NLP) models monitor message patterns, flagged keywords, and solicitation attempts related to underage interactions.
  • Restricted Peer-to-Peer Calling: WebRTC voice and video calls can only occur between mutually matched, verified adult users. Unsolicited calling or streaming to unverified accounts is technically restricted.
  • 24/7 Safety Review Team: Dedicated human safety specialists review flagged interactions, user reports, and automated safety triggers around the clock.

4. User Reporting Mechanisms (In-App & Web)

Every user on Winter has access to clear, immediate reporting tools built directly into the interface:

A. In-App Reporting

  1. Profile Reporting: Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) on any user profile → Select Report User → Choose the category "Underage User" or "Child Safety / Inappropriate Content" → Submit.
  2. Chat Reporting: Inside any direct conversation, long-press the offending message or tap the header menu → Select Report Message / User → Choose "Child Exploitation / Underage".
  3. Live Call Reporting: During or immediately following a WebRTC voice/video call, tap the Report button to immediately alert our emergency trust and safety queue.

B. Web & Direct Emergency Reporting

Reports can also be filed directly via our safety response email:

Emergency Safety Email: safety@winterdating.app

Subject Line: [URGENT CSAE REPORT] - User ID / Phone Number

Triage Priority: Handled within 1 to 4 hours by our high-priority Trust & Safety team.

5. Immediate Enforcement & Account Termination

Upon receiving any report or automated alert involving CSAE or CSAM:

  • Immediate Account Suspension: The reported account is locked instantly to prevent further communication or activity.
  • Permanent Ban: Upon confirmation, the account is permanently terminated. Associated device IDs, phone numbers, email addresses, and payment instruments are permanently blocklisted.
  • Content Eradication: All offending content is permanently purged from our public feeds and communications servers after evidentiary retention.
  • Evidence Preservation: Server logs, timestamps, IP addresses, transaction records, and communication metadata are securely archived under cryptographic tamper-proof protections for law enforcement submittal.

6. Mandatory Law Enforcement & NCMEC Reporting

Winter fulfills all legal obligations under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and applicable international statutes regarding child safety:

  • NCMEC CyberTipline Reports: We file formal, expedited reports with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) detailing the offender's profile details, IP addresses, timestamps, and preserved digital material.
  • Full Law Enforcement Cooperation: We proactively cooperate with state, federal, and international law enforcement agencies (including the FBI, Homeland Security Investigations, and INTERPOL) to facilitate apprehension and prosecution of offenders.

1. Who We Are

Winter ("Winter", "we", "us", or "our") is a modern dating platform designed to foster intentional connections, thoughtful conversations, and cozy romance.

The entity responsible for processing your personal information (the "data controller") under this Privacy Policy is Winter App Inc.

2. Where This Privacy Policy Applies

This Privacy Policy applies to the Winter mobile application, the Winter website (located at winterdating.web.app), and any related services, features, or content offered by Winter (collectively, the "Services").

3. Data We Collect

We cannot help you build meaningful connections without collecting certain information about you. We collect data in three main ways: information you choose to give us, information generated through your use of our services, and information from third parties.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Profile Information: When you create an account, you provide us with basic profile details including your name, gender, date of birth, dating preferences, biography, interests, and profile photographs.
  • Chats & In-App Messages: Text conversations, emoji reactions, and media shared between matched users to facilitate your connection.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: Details and correspondence you share when reaching out to our support or reporting an issue.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, app version, language settings, and temporary IP addresses used to establish peer connections.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Activity timestamps, feature interactions, profile views, match actions, and call session durations (metadata only, not audio/video streams).
  • Push Notification Tokens: Secure push tokens (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging) used exclusively to notify you of incoming calls, matches, and messages.

4. Why and How We Use Your Data

We process your data for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Delivering Core Services: Creating and managing your account, displaying your profile to compatible daters, and enabling real-time chats and calls.
  • Safety and Fraud Prevention: Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, harassment, impersonation, spam, and conduct that violates our Community Guidelines.
  • Managing Purchases & Wallet: Managing virtual call coin balances, talk-time credits, and purchase verifications.
  • Improving Features: Analyzing anonymized usage patterns to enhance system reliability and overall experience.
  • Legal Compliance: Complying with applicable laws, regulations, and legal requests.

5. How We Share Data

We believe your personal life is personal. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or advertisers. Data is shared only under specific circumstances:

  • With Other Users: When you publish profile information (such as your photos, bio, interests, and age), it becomes visible to potential matches within the service according to your discovery settings.
  • With Service Providers: Trusted cloud and infrastructure partners (such as Google Firebase for push notifications and hosting, TURN servers for call routing) operating under strict data protection terms.
  • Payment Processors: Official Google Play Billing or authorized payment gateways for coin purchases. Your payment card numbers and banking credentials are handled directly by the processors and never stored by Winter.
  • Legal & Safety Requirements: Where required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when necessary to protect personal safety or prevent illegal acts.

6. WebRTC Calling & Media Privacy

Winter provides live 1-on-1 audio and video calling through standard WebRTC peer-to-peer protocols:

  • Call media is transmitted directly between callers over encrypted DTLS-SRTP channels whenever network conditions allow.
  • Our relay servers (TURN) assist in routing encrypted packets through firewalls when direct peer connection is not possible. Our servers do not decrypt, listen to, record, or store your live audio or video conversations.
  • Calls never reveal your personal phone number or email address to the other party.

7. Biometric Security & Device Protection

Winter provides an optional Biometric Security Lock using the Android BiometricPrompt framework:

  • All biometric verification (fingerprint or face) takes place strictly inside your device's isolated hardware security module (Trusted Execution Environment / Secure Enclave).
  • Winter never accesses, collects, or transmits your raw biometric data. The device operating system simply informs the app whether the unlock was successful.

8. Your Rights & Choices

You are in control of your personal data. You have the right to:

  • Access Your Information: Review your profile details, chat history, and account settings directly in the app.
  • Correct or Update Details: Update your bio, photos, and preferences anytime in your Profile Settings.
  • Control Visibility: Toggle your discovery status or switch to Private Mode to pause showing your profile to new daters.
  • Delete Your Account: You can delete your account at any time directly through Settings > Account > Delete Account.

9. How Long We Retain Your Data

We keep your personal information only as long as you maintain an active account with Winter.

Upon account deletion, your profile is immediately taken down from discovery and search. Associated personal messages and profile data are completely purged from our active databases within 30 days, except where brief retention is required to fulfill statutory legal obligations or resolve safety reports.

10. Children's Privacy (Strict 18+ Only)

Winter is strictly for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not permit individuals under 18 to create accounts, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you suspect an underage user has created an account, please report it to our team immediately, and we will take prompt action to remove the account.

11. Data Security Safeguards

We employ rigorous administrative, physical, and technical measures to safeguard your personal data, including TLS 1.3 network transport encryption, authenticated WebSockets, encrypted server databases, and optional anti-screenshot flags on sensitive screens.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Because we are constantly evolving our services and safety features, this Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. When significant changes occur, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this document and provide appropriate notice through the app or website.
